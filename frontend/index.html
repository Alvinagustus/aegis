    AI review verdicts

    A reviewer account attests a risk score on-chain. No verdict → denied. Verdicts are bound to the exact transfer (chain, to, amount, memo hash, nonce), single-use and expire after a TTL.

    Guardian actions

    Signed by a guardian wallet.

    Panic freezes all spending at once. Only the owner can unfreeze it, after the cooldown.

    Pending admin changes

    Timelocked: guardians can veto, the owner applies after the delay.

    Holds & time capsules

    First-seen addresses are held for a quiet period; capsules seal funds until a date.
